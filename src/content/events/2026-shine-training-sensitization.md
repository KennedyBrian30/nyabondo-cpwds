# Sensitization Training on Disability Assessment and Early Identification

## Building Capacity for Inclusive Rehabilitation Services

<div class="event-intro">

The SHINE Project (Strengthening capacities for Holistic and Inclusive Need-based rehabilitative services that Empower individuals and communities), supported by Christian Blind Mission, is implementing a comprehensive capacity-building initiative across Kisumu and Homa Bay Counties from 2026 to 2029.

As part of this initiative, Nyabondo Centre conducted sensitization training sessions for rehabilitation officers, medical personnel, Community Health Assistants (CHAs), and Community Health Practitioners (CHPs) in both counties. The training aimed to strengthen disability assessment capabilities, improve early identification, and enhance referral pathways for persons with disabilities.

</div>

</div>

## A Collaborative Approach to Disability Assessment

The sensitization training brought together rehabilitation officers, medical personnel, CHAs, and CHPs from Kisumu and Homa Bay Counties. The training was conducted in two phases to ensure comprehensive coverage across both counties:

| Phase | County | Dates |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Phase 1 | Kisumu County | 28th – 31st July 2026 |
| Phase 2 | Homa Bay County | 4th – 7th August 2026 |

The training covered standardized disability assessment and categorization, early identification of disabilities, and the importance of timely intervention and referral to rehabilitation services.

<div class="event-highlight">
  <i class="fas fa-bullseye"></i>
  <div>
    <strong>Key Objective:</strong> To build the capacity of rehabilitation officers and medical personnel in standardized disability assessment and categorization in line with the new national guidelines, ensuring accurate and consistent assessments across the SHINE project implementation areas.
  </div>

## Training Objectives for Rehabilitation Officers and Medical Personnel

The training on disability assessment and categorization was designed to achieve the following objectives:

### 1. Build Capacity in Standardized Assessment
To build the capacity of rehabilitation officers and medical personnel in standardized disability assessment and categorization in line with the new national guidelines.

### 2. Improve Assessment Quality
To improve the quality, accuracy, and consistency of disability assessments conducted during SHINE project outreaches.

<div class="event-image">
  <img src="/images/events/shine-training-assessment.webp" alt="Disability assessment training session in Kisumu" />
  <p class="image-caption">The Administrator of Nyabondo Centre, Sr. Ludovena, together with trainers from the Ministry of Health National Rehabilitation Unit, the County Rehabilitation Coordinator for Kisumu, and the Nyabondo SHINE Programs Coordinator during the disability assessment training in Kisumu.</p>
</div>

### 3. Enhance Early Identification
To enhance early identification of persons with disabilities at community and facility levels through improved screening and detection protocols.

### 4. Strengthen Linkage to Services
To strengthen the linkage between assessment and service provision, including rehabilitation services, assistive devices, corrective surgeries, and therapy.

### 5. Improve NCPWD Registration
To improve registration of persons with disabilities with the National Council for Persons with Disabilities (NCPWD) through proper assessment and documentation.

    "We are building a foundation for inclusive rehabilitation services across Kisumu and Homa Bay. This training ensures that our frontline health workers have the skills they need to identify disabilities early and connect individuals to the right services."

  <cite>— Sr. Ludovena, Administrator, Nyabondo Centre</cite>

### 6. Equip with Practical Skills
To equip rehabilitation officers and medical personnel with practical skills and tools for conducting functional and medical assessments in both outreach and facility settings.

### 7. Strengthen Referral Pathways
To strengthen referral pathways and coordination among health facilities, community structures, and rehabilitation service providers to ensure seamless continuity of care.

### 8. Promote Rights-Based Approach
To promote a rights-based and inclusive approach to disability assessment and service delivery in Kisumu County and Homa Bay County.

## Sensitization for CHAs, CHPs, and OPDs on Early Identification

In addition to the training for rehabilitation officers and medical personnel, sensitization sessions were conducted for Community Health Assistants (CHAs), Community Health Practitioners (CHPs), and Outpatient Department (OPD) staff in both counties.

## Objectives of the Sensitization

The sensitization aimed to achieve the following objectives:

### 1. Raise Awareness on Early Identification
To raise awareness among CHAs, CHPs, and OPD staff on the importance of early identification of disabilities in children and adults at the community and facility levels.

### 2. Build Screening Capacity
To equip community health workers with basic screening skills for early detection of physical, sensory, and developmental disabilities.

### 3. Promote Prevention Awareness
To promote awareness of prevention strategies for disabilities, including maternal and child health interventions, immunization, and injury prevention.

### 4. Strengthen Referral Networks
To strengthen referral networks between community health workers, health facilities, and rehabilitation service providers.

<div class="event-highlight">
  <i class="fas fa-hands-helping"></i>
  <div>
    <strong>Community Impact:</strong> By training CHAs and CHPs, the SHINE Project is establishing a community-based early identification system that reaches persons with disabilities at the grassroots level, ensuring they receive timely intervention and support.
  </div>
</div>

### 5. Enhance Documentation and Reporting
To improve documentation and reporting of identified cases through proper screening tools and referral forms.

### 6. Foster Community Engagement
To foster community engagement and awareness on disability inclusion, reducing stigma and promoting acceptance of persons with disabilities.

### 7. Link Communities to Services
To enhance linkages between communities and available services, including rehabilitation services, assistive devices, and social protection programs.

## Key Outcomes

The sensitization training and workshops delivered the following key outcomes:

### For Rehabilitation Officers and Medical Personnel
- Improved knowledge and skills in standardized disability assessment and categorization
- Enhanced capacity to conduct functional and medical assessments accurately
- Stronger referral pathways between health facilities and rehabilitation service providers
- Better understanding of NCPWD registration processes and documentation requirements
- Increased confidence in identifying and managing disability cases

### For CHAs, CHPs, and OPDs
- Enhanced awareness of early identification and intervention strategies
- Improved screening and detection skills at the community level
- Stronger referral networks and community linkages
- Better understanding of disability prevention and inclusion
