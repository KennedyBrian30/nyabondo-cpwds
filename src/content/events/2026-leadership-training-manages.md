# Leadership and Management Training for Senior Managers in Rehabilitative Services

## Building Leadership Capacity for Inclusive Rehabilitation

<div class="event-intro">

Nyabondo Centre for Persons with Disabilities, in collaboration with **Strathmore University** and supported by the **SHINE Project** (Strengthening capacities for Holistic and Inclusive Need-based rehabilitative services that Empower individuals and communities), is organizing a specialized **leadership and management training program** for senior managers in rehabilitative services.

This capacity-building initiative is designed to equip senior managers with the skills, knowledge, and tools needed to lead effective rehabilitation programs and drive inclusive service delivery across Kenya.

## About the Program

The leadership and management training program is a key component of the SHINE Project's capacity-building initiatives. It recognizes that effective rehabilitation services require not only skilled clinicians but also strong leaders and managers who can:

- Develop and implement strategic plans for rehabilitation services
- Manage resources efficiently and effectively
- Build and lead multidisciplinary teams
- Drive quality improvement in service delivery
- Advocate for inclusive policies and programs
- Foster partnerships and collaborations

<div class="event-highlight">
  <i class="fas fa-graduation-cap" style="color: var(--primary);"></i>
  <div>
    <strong>Collaboration with Strathmore University:</strong> This training program leverages Strathmore University's expertise in leadership development and management education, combined with Nyabondo Centre's deep experience in rehabilitation services.
  </div>
</div>

## Target Audience

This training program is designed for:

- **Senior Managers** in rehabilitation service organizations
- **Program Coordinators** managing disability and rehabilitation programs
- **Heads of Rehabilitation Departments** in health facilities
- **County Rehabilitation Coordinators** and their deputies
- **Directors and Deputy Directors** of rehabilitation services
- **Managers of Disability Programs** in non-governmental organizations
- **Policy Makers** involved in disability and rehabilitation services

## Training Content

The program will cover a comprehensive range of topics essential for effective leadership in rehabilitative services:

### 1. Strategic Leadership in Rehabilitation

- Vision and mission development for rehabilitation services
- Strategic planning and implementation
- Change management and organizational transformation
- Stakeholder engagement and partnership building

### 2. Financial Management and Resource Mobilization

- Budgeting and financial planning
- Resource mobilization and fundraising
- Grant management and donor reporting
- Financial sustainability strategies

### 3. Human Resource Management

- Building and leading effective teams
- Staff development and capacity building
- Performance management and accountability
- Motivation and retention strategies

### 4. Quality Improvement and Service Excellence

- Quality assurance frameworks
- Monitoring and evaluation of rehabilitation services
- Clinical governance and patient safety
- Continuous quality improvement

### 5. Policy Development and Advocacy

- Disability policy and legal frameworks
- Advocacy strategies for inclusive services
- Engaging with government and stakeholders
- Promoting disability rights and inclusion

### 6. Partnership and Collaboration

- Building effective partnerships
- Multi-sectoral collaboration
- Community engagement and participation
- Network development and management

## Training Format

The program will utilize a blended learning approach to ensure maximum engagement and knowledge retention:

### 1. In-Person Sessions

- Interactive lectures and presentations
- Case studies and real-world scenarios
- Group discussions and workshops
- Practical exercises and simulations

### 2. Experiential Learning

- Site visits and field experiences
- Peer learning and networking
- Leadership mentoring and coaching
- Action learning projects

### 3. Online Components

- Pre-reading materials and resources
- Online discussions and forums
- Virtual networking and collaboration

### 4. Assessment and Feedback

- Pre- and post-training assessments
- Continuous feedback and evaluation
- Action planning and follow-up support

Participants completing this program will:

   - Improved ability to lead and inspire teams
   - Better strategic thinking and decision-making skills

   - Better financial and resource management

   - Enhanced monitoring and evaluation capabilities

3. **Expanded Professional Networks**
   - Connections with peers across the sector
   - Mentorship and coaching relationships
   - Collaborative partnerships and alliances

4. **Increased Organizational Impact**
   - Improved service delivery and outcomes
   - Enhanced organizational sustainability
   - Stronger advocacy and policy influence

5. **Personal Leadership Growth**
   - Increased self-awareness and confidence
   - Enhanced resilience and adaptability
   - Clearer vision and purpose
