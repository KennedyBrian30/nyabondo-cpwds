# Continuous Orthopaedic and Reconstructive Surgeries at Nyabondo Centre

## Restoring Mobility, Dignity, and Hope

<div class="event-intro">

Nyabondo Centre for Persons with Disabilities, through the SHINE Project (Strengthening capacities for Holistic and Inclusive Need-based rehabilitative services that Empower individuals and communities), supported by Christian Blind Mission, is proud to offer **continuous orthopaedic and reconstructive surgeries** for persons with disabilities across Kenya.

This ongoing program ensures that individuals with various orthopaedic conditions have access to life-changing surgical interventions, comprehensive rehabilitation, and assistive devices—all aimed at restoring mobility, function, and dignity.

## About the Program

The continuous orthopaedic and reconstructive surgery program at Nyabondo Centre is a core component of our rehabilitation services. Supported by the SHINE Project and funded by Christian Blind Mission, this program provides:

- **Corrective orthopaedic surgeries** for children and adults with physical disabilities
- **Reconstructive procedures** to restore function and appearance
- **Post-surgical rehabilitation** including physiotherapy and occupational therapy
- **Assistive devices** including prosthetics, orthotics, and mobility aids
- **Comprehensive follow-up care** to ensure optimal recovery

## Conditions We Treat

Our surgical team specializes in a wide range of orthopaedic and reconstructive conditions. We provide comprehensive care for both children and adults.

### Orthopaedic Conditions

Nyabondo Centre offers surgical interventions for the following orthopaedic conditions:

| Condition | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Clubfoot (Talipes Equinovarus)** | A congenital condition where the foot is twisted out of shape or position |
| **Cerebral Palsy-related deformities** | Musculoskeletal issues resulting from cerebral palsy, including contractures and gait abnormalities |
| **Post-burn contractures** | Scar tissue formation that restricts movement following burn injuries |
| **Congenital limb deformities** | Birth defects affecting the arms, legs, hands, or feet |
| **Fractures and malunions** | Broken bones that have healed improperly |
| **Osteomyelitis** | Bone infections requiring surgical debridement and reconstruction |
| **Hip dysplasia** | Abnormal development of the hip joint |
| **Knee deformities** | Conditions such as genu valgum (knock-knees) and genu varum (bow-legs) |
| **Spinal deformities** | Conditions like scoliosis and kyphosis |
| **Limb length discrepancies** | Unequal leg or arm lengths requiring correction |

### Reconstructive Conditions

Our reconstructive surgery services address:

| Condition | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Cleft lip and palate** | Congenital conditions affecting the lip and/or palate |
| **Post-traumatic deformities** | Physical deformities resulting from accidents or injuries |
| **Burn scar contractures** | Scar tissue that limits movement after burns |
| **Soft tissue defects** | Areas requiring skin grafts or flap reconstruction |
| **Hand deformities** | Congenital or acquired conditions affecting hand function |
| **Facial deformities** | Conditions affecting facial structure and appearance |

## The Surgical Process

### Step 1: Initial Assessment
Patients undergo a comprehensive medical evaluation by our multidisciplinary team, including orthopaedic surgeons, physiotherapists, and rehabilitation specialists. This assessment determines the most appropriate surgical intervention.

### Step 2: Surgical Planning
Our surgical team develops a personalized treatment plan tailored to each patient's specific condition, age, and overall health status.

### Step 3: Surgery
Surgeries are performed at Nyabondo Centre's fully equipped surgical facility by experienced orthopaedic and reconstructive surgeons.

### Step 4: Post-Surgical Care
Patients receive dedicated post-operative care, including wound management, pain control, and early mobilization.

### Step 5: Rehabilitation
A comprehensive rehabilitation program follows surgery, including physiotherapy, occupational therapy, and functional training to restore mobility and independence.

### Step 6: Assistive Devices
Patients requiring prosthetics, orthotics, or mobility aids receive custom-fitted devices from our assistive technology unit.

## How to Access Our Services

### Referral Process

Patients can access our surgical services through several pathways:

1. **Direct Contact:** Individuals can contact Nyabondo Centre directly for assessment and booking.
2. **Community Outreach:** Our outreach teams identify patients during community visits and refer them for surgical assessment.
3. **Health Facility Referral:** Patients can be referred by their local health facility or hospital.
4. **EARC Referral:** Education Assessment and Resource Centre (EARC) officers can refer children identified during school visits.

### Booking Information

To book a surgical assessment or procedure, please:

1. **Contact Nyabondo Centre** via phone, email, or in-person visit
2. **Provide relevant medical history** and any existing medical records
3. **Schedule an assessment** with our medical team
4. **Receive a surgical plan** and cost estimate (subsidized by the SHINE Project)
5. **Schedule your surgery** and prepare for admission

## Success Stories

Our continuous surgery program has transformed countless lives across Kenya. Here are just a few examples:

### Joyline's Journey
A young girl from Nyakach with clubfoot who could barely walk and faced stigma at school. After corrective surgery at Nyabondo Centre, Joyline now walks confidently, attends school regularly, and is reunited with her mother.

### Anjenjo's Success
A bilateral above-knee amputee who received prosthetics and rehabilitation support. Today, Anjenjo runs his own computer business and lives independently.

### Robert's Story
A young boy identified during a community outreach who underwent long-term therapy and formal schooling at Nyabondo Centre. Robert now works as a technician at the centre, giving back to the community that supported him.
